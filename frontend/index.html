What's in the guide

The essentials of the game, in plain language

The simplified Rules of Golf cover the situations you meet on a normal round. The assistant can help with topics like these.

  • Playing the ball

    How a hole is played from the teeing area to the hole, and playing the ball as it lies.

  • Penalty areas & relief

    When and how you can take relief, and where the ball must be dropped.

  • Lost ball & out of bounds

    What to do when your ball can't be found or comes to rest out of bounds.

  • Clubs & equipment

    Which clubs and balls you may use during a round, and how many you can carry.

  • On the putting green

    Marking, lifting and cleaning your ball, and what you can repair on the green.

  • Penalties explained

    The difference between a one-stroke penalty and the general penalty, and when each applies.
